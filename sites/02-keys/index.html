    Who this isn't for.

    We'd rather say it now than on the call. The programme works best when a cohort is at a similar stage.

    • You run 5–30 units and most of the operations still go through you.
    • You want a company with a team, not a bigger side income.
    • You can give it 3–4 hours a week for 12 weeks.
    • You want passive income without running a business.
    • You're looking for ways around local short-let rules.
    • You want someone to find and run the units for you.
    • You haven't hosted a stay yet.
