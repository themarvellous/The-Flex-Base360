12 weeks. 4 chapters. Each fixes one reason you're stuck.

  1. 01Weeks 1–3

  2. 02Weeks 4–6

    Operations

    Cleaning, check-in and guest messages that run without your phone.

Is it for you?

Honest about who it's for.

It's for you if

  • You run 5–30 units and most operations still go through you.
  • You want a company with a team, not a bigger side income.
  • You can give it 3–4 hours a week for 12 weeks.

It's not for you if

  • You want passive income without running a business.
  • You're looking for ways around short-let rules.
  • You haven't hosted yet.
