§07House rulesWho this is (and isn't) for

House
rules.

We'd rather tell you now than on the call. The programme works best when everyone in the cohort is at a similar stage.

Check-in

  • You run 5–30 units and most of the operations still go through you.
  • You want a company with a team, not a bigger side income.
  • You can give it 3–4 hours a week for 12 weeks.
  • You'll bring your real numbers to the 1:1s.

Not permitted

  • Looking for passive income without running a business.
  • Looking for ways around local short-let rules.
  • Wanting someone to find and run the units for you.
  • Never hosted a stay? and join the waitlist.

[Price] for the 12 weeks, including Base360 during the programme. Payment plans are available. We'll share the price before the call, so it isn't a surprise on it.

Q.How much time does it take each week?

About 3–4 hours: the 90-minute live session, plus time applying it to your own units. Sessions are recorded if you miss one.

Q.I'm not in London. Does it work where I am?

Yes. It's online, and the method runs in 7 cities across the UK, Europe and North Africa. Rules differ by city, so we teach you how to check yours rather than one country's law.

Q.What if it isn't right for me?

[Guarantee terms, e.g. a full refund after the first two sessions.]

Q.Do I have to use Base360?

No. It's included because it's what we use, but everything we teach works with the tools you already have.

Q.What happens on the strategy call?

20–30 minutes with the team. We look at your units, margins and goals, and tell you honestly whether the programme is the right next step. If it isn't, we'll say so.
