The problem

Most rental businesses stall between 5 and 30 units.

Demand isn't the limit. Your hours are. Every unit you add brings the same messages, cleans and pricing calls, and they all still land on you.

Stall 1

You are the operations team.

Every check-in, cleaner and 2am door-code message runs through your phone.

Stall 2

Revenue grows. Margin doesn't.

Spreadsheet pricing, double bookings and weak landlord terms eat the upside.

Stall 3

Each new unit adds work, not profit.

Without systems, unit 12 takes as much of you as unit 1 did.

The method

Twelve weeks, in the order we learned it.

Each part fixes one stall, and each ends with something you use the next Monday: a model, a document or a system that's already running.

Is it for you?

Who this isn't for.

We'd rather tell you now than on the call. The programme works best when the group is at a similar stage.

It's for you if

  • You run 5–30 units and most of the operations still go through you.
  • You want a company with a team, not a bigger side income.
  • You can give it 3–4 hours a week for 12 weeks.
  • You're happy to share your real numbers in 1:1s.

It's not for you if

  • You want passive income without running a business.
  • You're looking for a way around local short-let rules.
  • You want someone to find and run the units for you.
  • You haven't hosted a single stay yet.
