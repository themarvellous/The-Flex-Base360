    My recommendation

    Lead with C, prove it with A, dramatise the pain with B.

    1. C is the hero. The visitor answers three of the four qualifying questions just by reading the headline. Clicking "Book my free strategy call" carries those answers into the form, so the booking flow starts one step from done. This scores directly on the 25% conversion criterion.
    2. A becomes the "Who teaches it" section. The growth track is the strongest answer to "why should I trust these people?" It works better lower down, once the visitor has said where they are and can map their stage onto the founders' stages.
    3. B becomes "The problem" section. The before/after slider shows what "stuck at 5–30 units" feels like and hands off to the Base360 part of the story without turning the page into a software ad.
    4. The result is one page argument: where are you, here's why you're stuck, here's who solved it, here's how, book the call.
